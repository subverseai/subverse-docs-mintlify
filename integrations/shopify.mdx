---
title: 'Shopify'
description: 'Connect your Shopify store so agents and workflows can read and manage orders and products.'
---

**Connection type:** Credential

Connect your Shopify store to SubVerse. Agents can answer customer questions about orders and products, and your Functions and Orchestrations can create, update, and delete them. A Shopify event can also start an orchestration.

## What you can do

| Capability | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Answer customer questions** | Give an agent access to your store, so it can look up order status, product details, and availability. |
| **Manage orders and products** | Create, read, update, and delete orders and products from Functions and Orchestrations. |
| **React to store events** | Start an orchestration when something happens in your store, such as a new order. |

## Credential types

Choose the one that matches how you want to connect.

| Credential type | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Shopify Access Token API** | Connecting a single store you own. Recommended for most people. |
| **Shopify OAuth2 API** | Connecting through your own Shopify app, including several stores |
| **Shopify API** | Legacy private app credentials. Only for stores that already have them. |

## What you'll need

- A Shopify store, and admin access to it
- Permission to create apps in the store's admin, or a Shopify Partner account for OAuth
- Your store's subdomain, the part before `.myshopify.com`

<Tip>
  To install the SubVerse app in your Shopify store, see [Connect Shopify store](/integrations/shopify-setup).
</Tip>

<Note>
  Step-by-step setup is in the SubVerse dashboard: go to **Integrations → Shopify** and open the **Documentation** tab.
</Note>
