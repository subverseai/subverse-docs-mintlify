**Connection type:** Channel

Use your existing telephony provider, dialer, or PBX with SubVerse voice agents. A SIP trunk connects your phone numbers to SubVerse, so your agents can answer incoming calls and place outgoing calls on those same numbers.

## What you can do

| Capability | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Answer inbound calls** | Route calls from your numbers to SubVerse and pick the voice agent that handles them. |
| **Place outbound calls** | Let voice agents call customers from your numbers, using your provider's network and rates. |
| **Escalate to a human** | Transfer a live call to your agent queue or extension, with an optional call summary for context. |

## How it works

### Inbound

**Caller** → **Your SIP provider** → **SubVerse SIP server** → **Voice agent**

### Outbound

**Voice agent** → **SubVerse SIP server** → **Your SIP provider** → **Customer**

### Escalation

**Voice agent** → **SIP REFER** → **Your agent queue** → **Human agent**

Calls are secured with digest authentication (username and password). Your provider stays in control of numbers, routing, and billing.

## What you'll need

- A SIP trunk from any SIP-compliant provider, dialer, or PBX
- One or more phone numbers on that trunk, in international format
- A SIP username and password for the trunk
- Access to your firewall, to allow traffic from SubVerse
- A voice agent in SubVerse, to answer inbound calls
- A transfer destination (SIP URI or extension), if you plan to escalate calls
