The LLM understands the conversation, follows your prompt, calls functions and writes the agent's replies. Select one in the **LLM Model** field of any agent's configuration, for example the [Voice Agent](/agents/voice-agent-configuration) or [Chat Agent](/agents/chat-agent-configuration).
